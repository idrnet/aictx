## Test 2026-05-15 14:27

Adult male, ~50% dark brown + ~50% grey, applied to **scalp + beard** in one session.

**Result:** ✅ Natural result on both, no irritation.

## Notes

- **Not officially for beard** — patch-test first, rinse on any tingling.
- **No lifting** — only deposits tone. Won't lighten dark strands.
- **Fades evenly** in ~3-4 weeks; plan monthly re-application.
- One box covers short-hair head + beard → ~€10-13/month.

**Avoid confusing with:** Excellence Crème (permanent, ammonia) · Magic Retouch (washes out) · Casting Natural Gloss (different sub-line).
